# Competitive Experience Analysis

Compare products only from observable, dated evidence.

## Method

- Select direct, indirect, and analogous products with a stated rationale.
- Evaluate the same tasks across products: onboarding, consent, spending insight, budget guidance, transaction discovery, explanation, correction, and escalation.
- Record evidence source, access date, platform, region, and unavailable areas.
- Separate observation from interpretation and opportunity.
- Assess clarity, control, evidence visibility, privacy, accessibility, and failure recovery.

## Constraints

- Do not infer a feature from marketing language alone.
- Do not copy proprietary designs or text.
- Flag results that may vary by account, region, subscription, or release.

## Deliverable contract

Return a comparison matrix, evidence log, patterns, gaps, and Fin Buddy opportunities. Label it `DRAFT — REQUIRES HG-01 APPROVAL`.
