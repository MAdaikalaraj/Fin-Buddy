# Phase 01 Final Output — Understand

**INTERNAL PROVISIONAL — SPECIFICATION AND SYNTHETIC EVALUATION ONLY**

**Sharing:** Internal only. External publication requires separate human review and authorization.

## Phase result

The Understand phase established the research plan, evidence boundaries, competitive patterns, assumptions, unknowns, and fintech discovery risks for the Fin Buddy AI Assistant.

## Completed work

- Separated documented BRD facts from hypotheses and unknowns.
- Defined prioritized research questions for spending comprehension, budget questions, savings planning, consent, evidence, correction, trust, accessibility, and advice boundaries.
- Proposed eight moderated interviews using synthetic scenarios and no live financial data.
- Defined participant criteria, recruitment constraints, consent, redaction, retention, withdrawal, and analysis requirements.
- Created a non-leading interview guide and evidence thresholds.
- Reviewed Monarch Money, Rocket Money, Cleo, and Google Wallet using dated public sources.
- Identified opportunities for inspectable evidence, editable source data, correction, scoped permission, freshness, read-only boundaries, privacy controls, and explicit accessibility targets.
- Completed the Phase 01 fintech risk register.

## Evidence status

No interviews, observations, surveys, or usability tests occurred. No participant quotes or validated user findings exist. All user-problem statements remain hypotheses.

## Human decision

HG-01 was approved by the project owner with an explicit instruction to proceed provisionally. Later phases must not represent assumptions as validated research.
