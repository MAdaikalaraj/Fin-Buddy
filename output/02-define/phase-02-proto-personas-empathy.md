# Phase 02 — Proto-Personas and Provisional Empathy Maps

**Artifact state:** DRAFT_FOR_REVIEW — REQUIRES HG-02 APPROVAL  
**Evidence warning:** These are assumption-based proto-personas, not validated personas. No quotations, participant counts, or observed emotions are included.

## PP-01 — Routine Spending Reviewer

**Confidence:** Low  
**Basis:** BRD H1, H2, H4; spending visibility and transaction search; UC-01 and UC-03; BR-03–05 and BR-07.

### Provisional context and goals

- Periodically reviews balances or transaction history after a surprise or at month-end.
- Wants to understand where money went, why spending changed, and which transactions matter.

### Hypothesized needs

- Plain-language summary with an explicit period.
- Drill-down from totals to contributing transactions.
- Correctable categories, accounts, dates, and filters.
- Clear handling of stale, partial, or missing data.

### Hypothesized empathy map

| Dimension | Assumption to validate |
| --- | --- |
| Notices | Balance change, unusual category, or unfamiliar transaction |
| Questions | Which transactions caused this? Is the period and data complete? |
| May do | Search transactions, scan categories, compare periods |
| May be concerned about | Whether the total is current and whether data is reused beyond the question |
| Needs to verify | Period, accounts, categories, records, freshness, and correction effects |

## PP-02 — Household Budget Coordinator

**Confidence:** Low  
**Basis:** BRD H1–H4; budget awareness; UC-02 and UC-06; BR-02–08.

### Provisional context and goals

- Manages recurring categories, possibly across shared household responsibilities.
- Wants to know what remains, what changed, and whether the current pace is workable.

### Hypothesized needs

- Deterministic budget calculations.
- Explicit dates, accounts, categories, and assumptions.
- Ability to inspect, correct, or exclude transactions.
- Neutral, non-judgmental language without guaranteed outcomes.

### Hypothesized empathy map

| Dimension | Assumption to validate |
| --- | --- |
| Notices | Category approaching a limit or household spending increasing |
| Questions | How much remains? What changed? What context is missing? |
| May do | Recalculate, inspect shared expenses, adjust limits or plans |
| May be concerned about | Misclassification, judgmental advice, or broad data access |
| Needs to verify | Calculation, permission scope, transaction inclusion, and correction effect |

## PP-03 — Goal-Oriented Financial Planning Newcomer

**Confidence:** Low  
**Basis:** BRD H3 and H4; savings and literacy jobs; UC-04 and UC-05; BR-05–06 and MVP exclusions.

### Provisional context and goals

- Wants to build savings or understand a financial concept.
- Wants to translate a goal into a contribution and compare scenarios without committing to action.

### Hypothesized needs

- Editable assumptions and transparent deterministic calculations.
- Scenario comparison rather than one prescriptive answer.
- Clear education-versus-advice labeling.
- Uncertainty disclosure and safe escalation.

### Hypothesized empathy map

| Dimension | Assumption to validate |
| --- | --- |
| Notices | New goal, unfamiliar concept, or uncertainty about contribution size |
| Questions | What is required? Which inputs can change? Is this advice? |
| May do | Search, calculate, ask another person, or postpone planning |
| May be concerned about | Overconfident answers and hidden assumptions |
| Needs to verify | Inputs, scenarios, limitations, and advice boundary |

## Cross-profile tensions

- Speed versus evidence depth
- Personalization versus data minimization
- Proactive value versus intrusion
- Simple language versus accurate limitations
- Correct arithmetic versus imperfect categorization
- Conversation versus faster direct filters
- Contextual education versus regulated advice

These tensions are ideation inputs, not resolved design decisions.
