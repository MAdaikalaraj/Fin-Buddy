# Phase 02 Final Output — Define

**INTERNAL PROVISIONAL — SPECIFICATION AND SYNTHETIC EVALUATION ONLY**

**Sharing:** Internal only. External publication requires separate human review and authorization.

## Phase result

The Define phase converted the approved BRD and desk research into a provisional product definition, three assumption-based proto-personas, empathy hypotheses, journey maps, MVP priorities, success measures, and risk conditions.

## Provisional problem statement

Adults who actively monitor recurring finances may have difficulty turning transaction, category, and budget data into a timely explanation they can verify. Fin Buddy should test whether a consent-based, read-only conversational layer can reduce this interpretation effort through deterministic results, contributing records, explicit periods, assumptions, limitations, correction, accessibility, and clear advice boundaries.

## Proto-personas

- Routine Spending Reviewer
- Household Budget Coordinator
- Goal-Oriented Financial Planning Newcomer

These are low-confidence proto-personas—not validated personas. No invented quotations, prevalence, demographic capability assumptions, or validated emotions are included.

## Prioritized needs

- Explain spending and budget results with drill-down evidence.
- Find and correct source data.
- Understand data scope, freshness, permission, and retention.
- Receive clear limitations when intent or data is insufficient.
- Separate education, scenarios, and regulated advice.
- Support accessible, plain-language use.
- Provide editable savings assumptions and opt-in insights.

## MVP boundary

The MVP remains read-only. Payments, transfers, investing, loans, credit decisions, autonomous budget/account changes, personalized tax/legal advice, and unsupported real-time claims remain excluded.

## Human decision
