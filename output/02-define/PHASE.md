# Phase 02 — Define

## Purpose

Convert HG-01-approved evidence into a shared definition of users, problems, journeys, MVP needs, and success.

## Inputs

HG-01-approved evidence and the current BRD.

## Activities

Research synthesis, persona or proto-persona creation, empathy mapping, journey mapping, problem framing, use-case prioritization, and success-measure refinement.

## Review package

Themes, persona/proto-persona, empathy map, journey, prioritized pain points, problem statement, MVP definition, and unresolved questions.

## Exit condition

HG-02 is approved by a human. Drafts remain outside `output/`.
