# Phase 02 — Fintech Risk Review

**Artifact state:** DRAFT_FOR_REVIEW — REQUIRES HG-02 APPROVAL  
**Scope:** Provisional user definitions, journeys, MVP priorities, and success measures

| Risk | Severity | Required control for HG-02 |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Proto-personas or emotions presented as findings | Critical | Prominent assumption labels, BRD/source traceability, no invented quotes or prevalence claims |
| Demographic stereotypes used as capability proxies | High | Behavior/goal-based profiles and human stereotyping review |
| Financial vulnerability used to pressure engagement | High | Neutral language, dismissal, quiet/private modes, safe exit, no manipulative patterns |
| Education crosses into regulated advice | High | Preserve BR-06, jurisdiction review, no guarantees or protected-trait recommendations |
| Unnecessary personal/financial data is implied | High | Minimum data, purpose, consent, visibility, retention, and owner at each journey step |
| Consent reduced to one checkbox | High | Denial, withdrawal, scope change, permission management, deletion, and reduced-capability paths |
| AI assumed to calculate financial numbers | Critical | Deterministic services own arithmetic; AI explains retrieved/computed results |
| Answer cannot be inspected or corrected | High | Show period, filters, records, freshness, uncertainty, and correction paths |
| Accessibility deferred as polish | High | Treat as requirement and hypothesis; set target/owner; make no conformance claim yet |
| Support/escalation lacks ownership | High | Define trigger, message, handoff, shared data, response expectation, and owner |
| Engagement metrics reward unsafe behavior | High | Balance with correctness, grounding, consent, opt-out, accessibility, privacy, and harmful-answer guardrails |
| Future journey implies unconfirmed capabilities | High | Mark proposed behavior and dependencies; prohibit unsupported real-time claims |
| MVP expands into autonomous finance actions | Critical | Retain UC-08 and BRD Section 11 exclusions |
| Experience differs unfairly by language or context | Medium–High | Inclusive-language, numeracy, localization, assistive-tech, and bias validation |

## HG-02 minimum conditions

1. All profiles and empathy statements remain explicitly provisional.
2. Evidence provenance is visible at claim level.
3. Stereotyping and financial-vulnerability review is completed by a human.
4. Advice boundaries and MVP exclusions remain intact.
5. Consent, privacy, evidence, correction, deterministic calculations, accessibility, and escalation remain foundational.
6. Success measures balance usefulness with correctness, safety, privacy, accessibility, and control.
7. Participant research remains a validation dependency before production claims or final design commitment.
8. Unassigned Product, Legal/Compliance, Privacy/Security, Accessibility, Data/AI, and Support owners remain visible risks.
