# Fin Buddy UX Output Package

**INTERNAL PROVISIONAL — SPECIFICATION AND SYNTHETIC EVALUATION ONLY**

## Authorized contents

1. `Fin_Buddy_UX_Design_Specification.md` — provisional experience, architecture, interaction, component, accessibility, and prototype specification.
2. `Fin_Buddy_Synthetic_Evaluation_Report.md` — fictional-data arithmetic results and specification-based evaluation boundaries.
3. `Fin_Buddy_Risks_and_Next_Steps.md` — unresolved risks, owners to assign, and validation roadmap.
4. `01-understand/` — research planning, competitive analysis, risk review, phase definition, and HG-01 review.
5. `02-define/` — provisional product definition, proto-personas, empathy/journey artifacts, risk review, and HG-02 review.
6. `03-ideate/` — architecture, flows, concepts, risk review, phase definition, and HG-03 review.
7. `04-design/` — wireframes, high-fidelity specification, QA, risk reviews, CTA map, and HG-04 reviews.
8. `05-test/` — synthetic/specification evaluation, risk review, phase definition, and HG-05 review.

Each phase folder contains a canonical `FINAL_OUTPUT.md` summarizing the orchestrator-approved result and linking the supporting Markdown artifacts in that folder.

## Audience and purpose

Internal product, UX, engineering, privacy/security, accessibility, legal/compliance, analytics, and support stakeholders. Use this package for concept review, planning, feasibility assessment, rendered prototyping, participant research, and future implementation evaluation.

## Mandatory limitations

- No participant interviews or usability sessions occurred.
- No rendered or interactive prototype was evaluated.
- No live accounts or real personal/financial data were used.
- No production AI/model, API, financial-calculation service, authorization, retention/deletion, notification, support, or analytics implementation was tested.
- No assistive-technology, security, privacy, legal, or compliance validation occurred.
- Personas, needs, emotions, journeys, IA, designs, and content remain provisional.
- Synthetic arithmetic results do not establish production financial correctness.
- This package does not claim usability validation, accessibility conformance, security/compliance certification, or production readiness.

## Sharing

Internal only. External publication or reuse without these limitations requires separate human review and authorization.
