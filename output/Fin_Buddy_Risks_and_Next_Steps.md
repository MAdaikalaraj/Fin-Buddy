# Fin Buddy AI Assistant — Risks and Next Steps

**INTERNAL PROVISIONAL — SPECIFICATION AND SYNTHETIC EVALUATION ONLY**

## Mandatory limitations

- No participant research or rendered-prototype testing occurred.
- No live accounts or real personal/financial data were used.
- No production AI/model or integrated-service evaluation occurred.
- No assistive-technology, security, privacy, legal, or compliance validation occurred.
- No production validation or readiness assessment occurred.
- Synthetic arithmetic does not establish production financial correctness.

## High-priority unresolved risks

1. Consent duration, reuse, retention, deletion, and exception policies are unresolved.
2. Materiality rules for stale, partial, duplicate, conflicting, and unreconciled data are undefined.
3. Advice boundaries and qualified-support routes lack jurisdictional legal/compliance approval.
4. Prompt-injection protection exists as design intent, not implemented tool/data isolation.
5. End-to-end revocation/deletion cannot be verified across stores, logs, providers, replicas, and backups.
6. Proactive-insight triggers, thresholds, frequency, quiet hours, urgency, and suppression rules remain provisional.
7. No rendered or implemented accessibility evidence exists.
8. Product navigation, data/API availability, deterministic-service ownership, correction/undo, and support operations remain unconfirmed.

## Medium-priority risks

- Transaction matching and merchant normalization
- Financial wording that may imply causality or affordability
- Savings assumptions: interest, fees, inflation, irregular contributions, and rounding
- Privacy-minimized analytics and feedback schema
- Long evidence/thread navigation and screen-reader verbosity
- Localization of permissions, tasks, dates, currency, advice boundaries, and labels

## Next validation sequence

1. Confirm existing product screens, capabilities, data fields, APIs, freshness, and direct routes.
2. Assign owners and approve jurisdiction, currency, languages, accessibility target, consent, retention, deletion, advice, and support policies.
3. Build a rendered interactive prototype using synthetic data only.
4. Run automated/manual contrast review and the defined keyboard, focus, screen-reader, zoom/reflow, and reduced-motion matrix.
5. Conduct approved participant research and moderated usability testing.
6. Execute golden-dataset calculation and answer-grounding evaluations against implemented deterministic services.
7. Perform model/tool authorization, adversarial prompt-injection, unsafe-advice, privacy, retention/deletion, and security testing.
8. Verify direct/chat/evidence parity, CTA scope, correction propagation, and proactive-insight preference enforcement.
9. Resolve defects by severity and repeat the human gates before production decisions.
10. Obtain legal/compliance, privacy/security, accessibility, engineering, and operational launch approvals.

## Prohibited claims at this stage

Do not claim that Fin Buddy AI is user-validated, accessible/conformant, secure, compliant, production-ready, grounded against real records, correct on production data, or safe for live financial decisions.
