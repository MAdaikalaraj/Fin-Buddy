## Phase result

The Ideate phase selected a provisional dual-path hybrid architecture: direct finance experiences remain primary, contextual assistance supports visible tasks, and a global Ask Fin Buddy hub supports broader or multi-turn questions.

## Architecture principles

- Every assistant-supported task retains a direct non-chat route where the product supports it.
- Context, period, accounts, filters, and data scope are visible and editable before retrieval.
- General education works without personal financial-data consent.
- Retrieval, filtering, evidence, and financial calculations are deterministic.
- AI may interpret intent and explain supplied results but cannot alter financial values or invent causality.
- Correction of intent and correction of source data remain separate.
- Untrusted transaction text remains inert and cannot change permissions, tools, or scope.
- The MVP remains read-only.

## Use-case flows

- Monthly spending summary with evidence and correction
- Budget Q&A with formula and direct budget route
- Transaction search with visible filters and no-result recovery
- General education with a separate personalization transition
- Savings scenario with editable assumptions and no guarantee

## Required states

Consent lifecycle, stale/partial/missing/conflicting data, ambiguity, deterministic failure, restricted advice, malicious record text, correction, recalculation, history/deletion, permission withdrawal, feedback, support, accessibility, proactive insight opt-in, and safe exit.
