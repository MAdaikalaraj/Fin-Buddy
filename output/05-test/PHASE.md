# Phase 05 — Test

## Purpose

Determine whether users can complete priority tasks and whether the AI experience is understandable, accessible, correct, grounded, private, and safe.

## Inputs

HG-04-approved prototype and testable assumptions.

## Activities

Usability testing, accessibility review, financial-answer evaluation, grounding and safety evaluation, feedback synthesis, issue prioritization, iteration planning, and future-concept definition.

## Review package

Test plan, evidence, metric results, findings, severity-ranked issues, recommendations, conclusion, and future concepts.

## Exit condition

HG-05 is approved by a human. Only artifacts explicitly authorized in HG-05 may then be copied or promoted into `output/`.
