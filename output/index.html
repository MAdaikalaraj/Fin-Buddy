Live chatbot architecture

One question. Seven deliberate steps.

The FAQ chatbot checks safety first, uses the local FAQ as its factual source, and applies deterministic grounding and escalation rules at the end.

01 · INPUT

User question

A plain-language question enters the Fin Buddy chat.

02 · SAFETY

Pre-check

Blocks secret requests, unsafe actions, and prompt attacks before AI.

03 · CREWAI

Classify

Routes intent into an FAQ category or marks it unknown.

04 · CREWAI

Find FAQ facts

Searches the local 25-question FAQ and attaches source IDs.

05 · CREWAI

Draft answer

Writes a calm, concise response from supported facts only.

06 · CREWAI

Review & escalate

Checks whether a person should follow up; cannot override rules.

07 · FINAL CHECK

Validate & reply

Confirms sources, enforces policy, then returns the safe answer.

Grounding rule: answers come from the Fin Buddy FAQ. Unknown details remain marked for confirmation; the assistant does not invent product facts or provide personalized investment, tax, or legal advice.

UX lifecycle

From discovery to test

The lifecycle moves through five phases, with human review before meaningful progression and output authorization.

PHASE 01

Understand

Research plan, evidence boundaries, assumptions, and risks.

PHASE 02

Define

Provisional needs, personas, journeys, and MVP scope.

PHASE 03

Ideate

Concepts, flows, information architecture, and trade-offs.

PHASE 04

Design

Wireframes, high-fidelity specification, and prototype states.

PHASE 05

Test

Specification review, synthetic checks, limits, and next tests.
