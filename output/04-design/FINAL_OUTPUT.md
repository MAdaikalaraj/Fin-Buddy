# Phase 04 Final Output — Design

**INTERNAL PROVISIONAL — SPECIFICATION AND SYNTHETIC EVALUATION ONLY**

**Sharing:** Internal only. External publication requires separate human review and authorization.

## Phase result

The Design phase produced 30 annotated structural wireframes, traceability across UC-01–05 and BR-01–10, a provisional high-fidelity design specification, component and state requirements, a CTA scope map, prototype routes, accessibility intent, proactive-insight opt-in, and fintech risk reviews.

## Key design decisions

- Direct screens remain primary; assistant hub and contextual entry share rules.
- Deterministic financial result, AI explanation, evidence, freshness, and limitations are visually and semantically distinct.
- Evidence is reachable without another AI question.
- `Change question` does not modify source data; `Correct data` uses a confirmed canonical service.
- Consent choices remain neutral, optional scopes begin off, and denial preserves useful routes.
- Permission withdrawal, conversation deletion, history deletion, and account-data requests remain separate.
- Transaction descriptions render as inert untrusted data.
- Restricted financial advice and excluded actions fail safely.
- Proactive insight cards require PI-00–PI-06 opt-in and control flow.

## Design-system intent

- Calm, neutral, information-first hierarchy
- Provisional semantic colors, typography, spacing, and density
- WCAG 2.2 AA contrast intent
- Keyboard, focus, screen-reader status, zoom/reflow, non-color, reduced-motion, and direct-control requirements
- Responsive mobile, tablet, and desktop behavior

## Prototype scope

Contextual spending, hub budget, transaction search, education, savings, proactive insights, consent lifecycle, data failures, restricted advice, malicious text, privacy/deletion, feedback/support, accessibility behavior, and exact return-to-origin.
