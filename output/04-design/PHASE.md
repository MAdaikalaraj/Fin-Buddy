# Phase 04 — Design

## Purpose

Turn the approved experience architecture into an accessible, testable interface and conversational prototype.

## Inputs

HG-03-approved flows and state inventory.

## Activities

Annotated wireframes, wireframe review, high-fidelity specifications, content design, interaction states, accessibility review, and prototyping.

## Internal hold point

Stop after wireframes. High-fidelity work requires a human approval in HG-04 Checkpoint A.

## Review package

Wireframes, design decisions, content/conversation specification, high-fidelity design, prototype coverage, accessibility notes, and known limitations.

## Exit condition

HG-04 is approved by a human. Drafts remain outside `output/`.
